# CJ-Tech Management System

Welcome to the CJ-Tech Management System! This website is built specifically to help our tech shop manage repair jobs, technician schedules, and business performance easily.

## How to Access the System

1. **Open your web browser** (like Google Chrome, Safari, or Edge).
2. Type in the website link provided by the administrator (e.g., `http://cjtech.com`).
3. You will see the **Systems Access** login page on the right side.
4. Enter your "Terminal ID / Email" and your "Access Key" (Password).
5. Click the **Log In** button. 
   - *If you don't have an account, click "Register New Account" at the bottom to sign up.*

## Dashboard (Home)
Once logged in, you will land on the Dashboard. This page gives you a quick snapshot of the shop's health today:
- **Total Earnings:** See how much revenue has been collected from completed/active jobs.
- **Active Jobs:** See how many devices are currently being repaired.
- **Recent Activity:** Quick logs of the most recent job updates.
- **Scheduled Today:** A list of technicians and their appointments for the current day.

## Using the Job Orders
To manage repair jobs, click the **Job Orders** tab on the left sidebar.
- **Adding a new repair:** Click the blue **+ New Job Order** button at the top right. Fill in the customer name, device, issue description, and set the estimated price.
- **Viewing all repairs:** You can scroll through the list of devices being worked on. You can use the search bar to find a specific customer or device.
- **Updating a repair:** Click on a row to open its full details, where you can easily find its reference ID and see its status changes.

## Using the Schedules
To manage technician appointments, click the **Schedules** tab on the left sidebar.
- You will see a Calendar view. Days with blue dots have recorded appointments.
- To schedule a new appointment, click **+ New Appointment**. Fill in the client, assigned technician, and select the date and time.
- Click on any scheduled appointment pill on the calendar to see the full details.

## AI Assistant
We have an integrated AI Assistant! Click **AI Assistant** on the sidebar to chat with it. It knows all about your active job orders and schedules, so you can ask it questions like:
- "How many High Priority jobs do we have right now?"
- "What is our estimated total revenue?"
- "Who is Marcus repairing today?"

## Settings
In the **Settings** page, you can update your account details, switch the visual theme (between Dark Mode and Light Mode, although Dark Mode is recommended!), and safely log out of the system.

*(If you ever forget your password, simply click "Lost Access?" on the login page.)*
